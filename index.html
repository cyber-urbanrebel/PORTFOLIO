About Me

Who I Am

I’m Edwin Mburu Kamau — an IT & Cybersecurity Expert, serial entrepreneur, brand designer, professional artist, and competitive athlete based in Kenya. I operate at the intersection of technology, creativity, and business.

As the founder of SOPHIA (AI & cybersecurity solutions), Urban Crew Apparel, Revhood Automotive, and Brotherhood Fitness, I’ve built ecosystems spanning digital security, fashion, automotive culture, and sport.

My philosophy: mastery is not a destination — it’s the daily commitment to excellence in every lane you choose to run.
